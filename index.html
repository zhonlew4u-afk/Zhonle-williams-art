The artist

About Zhonle Williams

Texas painter, bold colour

Zhonle Williams is a Texas-based painter working in acrylic, oil, and spray paint on canvas. His work moves between floral studies, landscapes, city scenes, and expressive abstracts, united by bold colour and a close attention to the natural world.

His collection of 35 original paintings includes works such as Snowdrops by the Lake, a large 48 × 36 acrylic, alongside smaller floral and landscape pieces. Prices range from $300 to $2,500.

Paint is laid on thick and left honest — petals are sculpted rather than drawn, water is dragged and scraped until it shimmers, and dark grounds make every colour placed on them glow.

“Texas painter Zhonle Williams creates bold, original acrylic, oil, and spray paint works on canvas — floral studies, landscapes, and expressive abstracts.”
